How to calculate your DBR, step by step

1. Add your monthly loan payments

Include every EMI: personal, auto, home and any other loan.

2. Add the counted share of card limits

Banks count a percentage of each credit card limit, not the balance you owe. At 5%, a ৳2,00,000 limit adds ৳10,000.

3. Divide by net monthly income

Example: ৳12,000 EMI + ৳10,000 card share = ৳22,000. On ৳1,00,000 net income the DBR is 22%.

4. Compare with the cap

At a 50% cap you can add up to ৳28,000 of new monthly payments.

Bangladesh FAQ

Is DBR a Bangladesh Bank rule?

Bangladesh Bank’s consumer financing rules require each bank to set eligibility parameters in its own product program guidelines. A DBR limit is part of those, so the exact cap differs by bank, commonly 40–70%.

Which income counts?

Banks mostly use verified net income: salary after deductions, plus rent or business income they can document.

What is the longest personal loan tenure?

Five years (60 months) under the consumer financing regulations.

What is the unsecured credit card limit?

The regulation set ৳10 lakh per borrower for unsecured limits, with more only against liquid security. Bangladesh Bank revised several consumer financing limits in May 2026, so confirm the current figures with your bank.

Does a good DBR guarantee approval?

No. Banks also check your CIB report, job stability, age at loan maturity, and collateral where needed.

How can I lower my DBR?

Close or reduce card limits, repay small loans early, add a documented co-income, or choose a longer tenure.
